Support & Troubleshooting

Need help setting up Auto Planner 2027, activating a Pro license, or troubleshooting a sync? Start with the steps below or email einkpapergrid@gmail.com.

Quick Start

  1. Open any Google Sheet and go to Extensions → Auto Planner 2027 → Sync now to initialize your e-ink planner tabs.
  2. Open Connect sources… to choose Google Calendars, paste read-only .ics URLs (Outlook / iCloud), or link external task Sheets.
  3. Click Start syncing to enable automatic background updates.

How to Activate Your Pro License

  1. Purchase a one-time license key ($19) at einkpapergrid.gumroad.com/l/auto-planner-2027.
  2. Copy your license key from your Gumroad receipt email.
  3. In Google Sheets, open Extensions → Auto Planner 2027 → License & Upgrade…, paste your key, and click Activate License.

Troubleshooting

Events or tasks look out of date?
Run Extensions → Auto Planner 2027 → Sync now. If a sheet layout looks misaligned after manual edits, run Rebuild views from the add-on menu.

ICS feed not updating?
Confirm the URL starts with https:// (replace webcal:// with https://) and is a public/secret read-only .ics link.

Pocket View shows an authorization error?
Open Pocket View (phone app)… in the add-on menu and click Rotate token to generate a fresh private link.

Contact Studio Support

Email einkpapergrid@gmail.com with your Google Sheets error message (if any) and we’ll respond as quickly as possible.
