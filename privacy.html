Privacy Policy

Auto Planner 2027 is a Google Sheets Editor Add-on published by E-Ink Papergrid. We built Auto Planner 2027 so your personal schedule, tasks, and habits stay strictly inside your own Google account.

Google API Services User Data Policy — Limited Use Disclosure

Auto Planner 2027’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Zero Third-Party Storage of Your Google Data

E-Ink Papergrid does not operate any external server, database, or analytics service that collects, stores, or processes your Google Calendar events, Google Sheets content, Google Forms responses, or email address. All sync logic runs inside Google Apps Script within your own Google account, and all planner data is written directly to your own Google Spreadsheet.

2. Google OAuth Scopes Used & Why

Auto Planner 2027 requests only the permissions required to perform user-initiated planning features:

Scope Purpose
spreadsheets Reads and formats your active planner workbook and any Linked Google Sheets you explicitly add in Connect sources….
calendar.readonly Reads events from your selected Google Calendars to populate Today, Week, Month, and Year views. Never modifies or deletes events.
forms Creates two private Google Forms linked to your spreadsheet only when you click Create habit & task forms.
script.external_request Fetches read-only .ics calendar URLs you configure, and verifies your Gumroad license key against api.gumroad.com.
script.scriptapp Installs and removes time-driven triggers for automatic background sync when you enable Start syncing.
script.container.ui Renders the add-on dialogs inside Google Sheets.
script.send_mail & userinfo.email Used only when you click Email Pocket View link to me to send your private Pocket View URL to your own email address.

3. Gumroad License Verification

When you activate a Pro license key, Auto Planner 2027 sends only the license key you entered (and product identifier auto-planner-2027) to Gumroad’s license verification API (https://api.gumroad.com/v2/licenses/verify). No spreadsheet data, calendar events, or Google account identifiers are transmitted to Gumroad. Your license status and 7-day trial timestamp are cached locally in Google Apps Script UserProperties.

4. Data Sharing, Sale & AI Training

  • We never sell, rent, or transfer your Google user data to third parties.
  • We never use Google Workspace API data to train generalized AI or machine-learning models.
  • We never serve third-party advertisements or tracking pixels inside the add-on or Pocket View.

5. Revoking Access & Deleting Data

You can revoke Auto Planner 2027’s permissions at any time from your Google Account Security Permissions page, or uninstall the add-on via Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons inside Google Sheets. Deleting your planner spreadsheet removes all synced planner data.

For privacy questions or data inquiries, contact E-Ink Papergrid at support@applicantpackager.com.
